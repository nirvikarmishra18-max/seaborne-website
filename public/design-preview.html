We started as a small ship agency and grew by solving the problems our clients kept bringing back to us: finding a yard slot, sourcing a part at short notice, or getting a sale across the line.

Today the team covers vessel sale and purchase, technical management, drydock planning and marine supply. Most clients use two or three of those services together, which is where the single desk saves them time.

Our superintendents have sailed and managed ships themselves. They read a repair list the way a chief engineer does and price it the way an owner would.

We keep the network deliberately small. Every yard and supplier we recommend is one we have worked with directly and would send our own vessels to.
